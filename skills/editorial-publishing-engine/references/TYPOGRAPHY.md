# Typography v1.1

Natural Chinese reading comes first.

- Cover display: strong serif, semantic line breaks.
- Essay headline: clearly below cover display.
- Body: stable measure and line height; do not shrink merely to fit.
- Section marker: two-digit number + black section title; visually stronger than old kicker but weaker than headline.
- Maximum two font families per page.
- Chinese title breaks follow meaning, not equal character counts.
- Avoid giant full-sentence headlines when the sentence reads better as prose.
- English and Greek terms may remain visible when they are part of the lesson, but they must not become decorative badges.
