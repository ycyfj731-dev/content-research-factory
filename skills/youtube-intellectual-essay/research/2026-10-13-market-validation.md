## Editorial whitespace and hypotheses
Rejected commodity promise: "Everyone Is a Narcissist" or "Why Therapy Speak Is Ruining Society."
Stronger proposed argument: "As clinical vocabulary goes mainstream, it both helps people recognize harms and risks transforming descriptions of specific behaviors into fixed judgments about identities." 
Distinct element worth verifying: the China-specific evolution of PUA from pick-up artist to interpersonal/workplace manipulation, separated from the clinical diagnostic category. Avoid claiming all terms are clinical: patriarchy is a social theory term; PUA is an imported acronym with separate semantic history.
Title hypotheses (UNTESTED):
1. Why Do We Diagnose Everyone Now?
2. When Did Every Bad Behavior Become a Disorder?
3. The Words That Changed How We Judge People
Titles 1/2 risk conflating labels with actual diagnosis; they cannot pass Packaging Gate without explicit script qualification.

## Decision and blocking items
Topic feasibility: plausible. Observed adjacent viewership: yes, on mature channels. Direct competitor overlap: strong. Unproven: entrants' success, title advantage, 10–15m direct competition, video-level completeness, audience retention/CTR, monetization, small-channel breakout evidence.
Decision **REVISE**, not GO. Require 8–12 directly inspected relevant YouTube videos from >=4 channels, observed_at timestamps, individual canonical URLs/video IDs, publish dates, duration, actual verified title/thumb opening promise; distinguish relevant vs adjacent. Record search query, exclusion reasons and small-channel counterexamples. Document/source research & final thesis evidence remain separately BLOCKED.
