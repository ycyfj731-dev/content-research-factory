# V0.2 Engine Contracts — YouTube Intellectual Essay
Version: 0.2.0. Status: normative. Applies to Stages 01–07 and post-publication Stage 14.
All episode artifacts carry `schema_version`, `updated_at`, `input_versions`, `state`, and provenance. States: DRAFT/PASS/REVISE/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN; never conflate a planning PASS with video READY.

## A. Discovery Engine — real competitors and topic validation (Stages 01–02, before Stage 04)

**Inputs:** audience problem (one sentence); target market and language; 3–6 search phrases; observed YouTube video URLs and retrieval dates; topic hypotheses. Optional: authorized channel analytics. Offline mode is allowed but cannot claim observed demand.

**Outputs:** `01-topic.md` with audience job-to-be-done and explicit thesis opportunity; `02-competitors.md` with observation ledger; `02-opportunity.md` with competing explanations, differentiation and GO / REVISE / BLOCKED decision.

Observation ledger minimum fields per video: `video_id,url,channel,title,published_at,observed_at,duration_seconds,views_at_observation,search_query,similarity,opening_promise,central_claim,format,distinctive_visuals,unanswered_question,source_method`. Never infer hidden CTR, retention, demographics, RPM or impressions from public views. Never claim a historical view velocity from a single snapshot. Preserve URLs and observation times; capture search position only as a timestamped query observation, not a stable rank.

**Sampling protocol:** collect 8–12 directly relevant videos, where feasible, from at least 4 distinct channels; include both large and smaller channels; exclude and log irrelevant hits; separately mark adjacent-topic inspirations. Sample is a coverage heuristic, not proof of market demand. Extract no more than short fair-use analytical excerpts; do not clone scripts or creative packaging. Keep `sample_count`, `channel_count`, missing fields and search limitations explicit. If only 3–7 relevant observations exist, mark PARTIAL research and do not represent sample as comprehensive.

**Opportunity evaluation:** record audience tension, clarity of question, evidence feasibility, genuine new contribution, visual feasibility, production feasibility (0–4 or UNKNOWN following existing Topic Gate), plus a separate `observed-demand` judgment with evidence and uncertainty. A score may not replace a reasoned decision; enforce original Topic Gate floor and single-dimension hard failures. `GO` requires inspectable relevant competitor evidence, plausible source coverage, a falsifiable differentiation statement and an audience-facing problem. Otherwise `REVISE` or `BLOCKED`. Public view counts alone are not a demand forecast.

**Packaging pretest:** propose 3 materially distinct title/thumbnail/hook promises; identify what each promises and the evidence needed to fulfill it. Label all as UNTESTED until actual YouTube analytics/A-B results exist. No fabricated CTR predictions.

## B. Intellectual Engine — originality and counterarguments (Stages 03, 05, 06)

**Inputs:** source/claim ledger, original observation, competing mechanisms, preliminary thesis and real-world examples.

**Outputs:** `03-argument-map.md` and updated `03-sources.md` / `06-evidence.md`. Minimum schema:
- `THESIS`: one contestable sentence, scope, and what would change the conclusion.
- `INSIGHT`: one concrete authorial inference not merely a source paraphrase; labeled interpretation and separated from observed fact.
- `CLAIMS[]`: stable C-IDs, exact wording, type (empirical/textual/interpretation/hypothetical), source locator, verification state, confidence bounds and dependent B-/A-IDs.
- `RIVALS[]`: at least two serious alternative explanations, strongest supporting evidence, how they could coexist with the thesis and proposed discriminating evidence.
- `BOUNDARIES`: population, context, historical exceptions, uncertainty and what is not claimed.

**Originality check:** compare with observed competitor thesis and framing. Produce a difference statement that identifies the changed mechanism, evidence, perspective or limitation—not a mere new title. Paraphrased summaries, invented personal experiences and unsupported counterintuitive claims fail.

**Counterargument Gate:** PASS only when the strongest alternative is articulated fairly and either changes the thesis, is integrated, or remains an explicit unresolved limitation. REVISE for strawmen, binary framing, unearned causal leaps or a named theory treated as empirical proof. BLOCKED when a core empirical claim lacks inspectable support. Preserve existing Evidence Gate: all substantive spoken/chart/thumbnail claims must PASS before final narration/render.

## C. Narrative Engine — reasoning as visual story (Stages 04–07)

**Inputs:** approved argument map, audience tension, evidence ledger, title promise, target duration.

**Outputs:** `05-beats.md` + `05-script.md` + `07-av.md`. For every beat store `beat_id,viewer_question,change_in_understanding,evidence_claim_ids,visual_argument,estimated_seconds,transition_or_open_loop,exit_test`. Use flexible structure, not obligatory cliffhangers:
1. concrete contradiction or recognizable scene; establish stakes without overclaim;
2. initial explanation; identify why it is insufficient;
3. evidence/mechanism with an observable model;
4. strongest complication or rival account;
5. updated synthesis and callback that answers the opening promise.
Order may differ when justified. Each beat must either add evidence, change interpretation, create an evidence-based question or resolve one; filler fails.

**Hook test:** the first 30 seconds must define a compelling real question and not make a claim the video cannot substantiate. Mark speculative scenes as illustrative. No promises of universal answers based on one study.

**Retention preflight:** annotate estimated disengagement risks and corrective edits (not fictional retention data). Remove repetitive abstractions, name-drops, title restatements, empty suspense and stock-montage filler. Read the script aloud for natural English; respect ~10–15 min baseline only after actual measured VO. Every visual must perform explanatory work and reference a claim/beat. Existing visual/Anti-Slop and rights gates remain binding.

**Narrative Gate:** PASS if each beat has a meaningful state change, opening/ending deliver the packaging promise, rival account affects the conclusion, pacing has editorial variation, and no critical unsupported claim is spoken. Otherwise REVISE; BLOCKED if source dependencies prevent a factual narrative.

## D. Learning Engine — measured post-publication iteration (Stage 14, new; no automatic publication)

**Inputs:** explicit published `video_id`, publish date, snapshots of owner-authorized YouTube Studio analytics and the matching final artifact versions. Without channel data keep unavailable fields NULL, NEVER zero.

**Outputs:** `14-performance.md`, append-only `14-metrics.csv`, `14-experiments.md` and `14-lessons.md`.
Snapshot fields: `video_id,as_of,age_days,impressions,impressions_ctr,views,watch_time_hours,avg_view_duration_seconds,avg_percentage_viewed,retention_30s,traffic_source_mix,returning_viewers,subscriber_change,analytics_source,coverage_notes`. Note that some fields/aggregation are unavailable for small or new channels. Use comparable time windows (e.g. 48h/7d/28d after publish) instead of mixing different ages. Never backfill estimated values as measurements. Owner-only analytics are NOT public competitor metrics.

**Causal discipline:** register a hypothesis before a new upload (topic, hook, title, thumbnail, pacing), one primary metric and guardrails. Small sample and confounders mean results are descriptive, not proof. Title/thumbnail changes, impressions source shifts, paid promotion and algorithmic distribution must be logged. When available, use native YouTube thumbnail/title tests and report their actual criterion rather than inventing A/B significance. Do not retroactively rewrite prior measurements or claim a counterfactual.

**Learning Gate:** PASS if time windows, provenance, missingness, hypothesis and action are traceable; REVISE for uncomparable samples or overstated causal inference; BLOCKED when the report purports to contain analytics without real access. Lack of analytics => NOT_RUN (not a failed published video). Feed one concrete learning rule, with confidence label, to next `01-topic.md`, not an automatic change to all future creative choices.

## Cross-engine dependency rules

- Competitor findings altered -> invalidate opportunity, packaging and distinctiveness checks; keep separately verified sources unless impacted.
- Thesis/counterargument altered -> rerun Evidence + Narrative Gates; recheck VO, visuals, captions and metadata if already produced.
- Hook/title changed -> rerun promise/first-30-seconds consistency; do not reapprove a mismatched thumbnail automatically.
- Script changed -> recompute spoken claim IDs, VO timing, AV, edit, subtitles, chapter times.
- Analytics changed -> append snapshot; re-evaluate insight only, do not rewrite past source or production gates.
- A Stage 14 PASS means analysis is sound, not success or revenue. Stages 01–13 retain production readiness authority.

## Minimal execution recipe
1. Collect dated, directly linked competitor observations and inspect sources; create 01/02/03 artifacts.
2. Apply Discovery GO and Counterargument PASS; then approve packaging; draft beat map and script.
3. Run existing Evidence Gate before narration; run Narrative Gate and planned visual Anti-Slop before production.
4. Execute existing asset, rights, voice, edit, thumbnail, metadata and actual-render QA.
5. After authorized publication, record metrics snapshots, learnings and input-locked experiment notes. No automatic uploader, paid media or data extraction is implied.
