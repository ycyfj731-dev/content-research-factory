# Visual QA

A visual passes only if all applicable checks pass.

## Argument
- Does the frame help the viewer understand a relation, source, scene or shift?
- Would removing it weaken comprehension?
- Is the visual purpose more specific than “make this interesting”?

## Evidence
- Are real sources identifiable?
- Are numbers/quotes faithful?
- Is a reconstruction labeled?
- Does the visual imply more certainty than the evidence supports?

## Anti-PPT
- Is the layout avoiding default symmetric card-grid logic?
- Are arrows/boxes doing real explanatory work?
- Could this be mistaken for a corporate deck slide?
- Is there enough negative space and hierarchy?

## Anti-AI
- Are generated faces or generic cinematic scenes doing work a real/source/original graphic could do better?
- Is there suspicious fake text?
- Is the frame over-polished relative to the claim?
- Does texture look intentional rather than synthetic?

## Clinical / social concepts
- Are labels visually separated from diagnoses when necessary?
- Are people not reduced to categories?
- Is ambiguity preserved where the argument requires it?
- Are causal arrows avoided when only association or interpretation is supported?

## Motion
- Does each movement correspond to an argumentative change?
- Is the frame allowed to hold?
- Are key qualifiers on screen long enough to read?
- Are repeated entrance animations becoming mechanical?

## Channel identity
- Does annotation appear as a thinking tool rather than decoration?
- Does the visual belong to the same grammar as earlier frames?
- Is the identity coming from recurring rules rather than repeating one template?

## Video-specific acceptance

- Is each shot visually additive instead of repeating the subtitle?
- Do one- and two-line subtitles leave data, citations, scene text and faces legible?
- Does direct seek to any frame reproduce the same composition as sequential playback?
- Were start/mid/end transition frames actually inspected for missing text, overlap and clipping?
- Does the mascot use the approved reference asset and preserve identity?
- Are illustrative comment examples labeled and never misrepresented as actual accounts?
- Without a rendered asset, report motion and caption QA as NOT_RUN, never PASS.

## Final decision

Use:
- PASS
- REVISE
- REJECT

If REVISE, state the exact repair before regenerating or rebuilding.
