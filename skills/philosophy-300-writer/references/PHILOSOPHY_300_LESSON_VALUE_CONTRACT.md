# Philosophy 300 — Lesson Value Contract v1.0

Status: **FROZEN SERIES-LEVEL CONTRACT**

Purpose: define what every Philosophy 300 lesson must deliver to a zero-baseline reader beyond factual correctness.

This contract applies to the entire series, not only Lecture 002.

---

## 1. One lesson, one central question

Each lesson must answer one explicit central question.

The title controls the article.

Supporting questions may appear only when they help answer the central question.

Do not let the lesson drift into:
- a different source problem;
- a different philosopher;
- a different philosophical concept;
- background material that becomes the new subject.

If a paragraph is accurate but does not advance the central question, move or delete it.

---

## 2. Every lesson must deliver at least three kinds of value

### A. KNOW — what did the reader actually learn?

By the end, the reader must be able to state at least one specific fact, distinction, or conclusion they did not know before.

Bad:
> 我了解了一些背景。

Good:
> 我知道为什么泰勒斯常被放在西方哲学史的最前面。

---

### B. CORRECT — what misunderstanding was corrected?

Each lesson should correct at least one real, relevant misunderstanding when such a misunderstanding genuinely exists.

Do not manufacture “contrarian” claims for attention.

Example for Lecture 002:
> “第一位哲学家”不等于我们已经证明泰勒斯是人类历史上第一个进行哲学思考的人。

This is the lesson's correction value.

---

### C. TOOL — what can the reader reuse later?

Each lesson should leave the reader with at least one reusable way of reading, distinguishing, questioning, comparing, or reasoning.

Example for Lecture 002:
> 以后看到“某某古代哲学家认为……”，先问：这是他自己的原文，还是后人的转述？

This is the lesson's save value.

If a lesson leaves no reusable intellectual tool at all, it must justify why the lesson is still worth saving.

---

## 3. Person lessons require a Minimum Sufficient Portrait

If a philosopher's name appears in the title, a zero-baseline reader must finish the lesson knowing who that person is in the minimum amount necessary for the current question.

Minimum Sufficient Portrait may include:
- approximate period;
- place;
- why this person appears here;
- what survives / does not survive;
- relation to the lesson question.

This is not a biography.

Rule:
> If the reader finishes the lesson but still cannot say who this person was or why they matter here, the lesson fails.

---

## 4. Every lesson must earn interaction through content

Do not ask for likes, saves, or follows as a substitute for value.

The lesson itself must create reasons for action.

### LIKE REASON
The reader learned something specific, reliable, and worth acknowledging.

### SAVE REASON
The lesson contains a distinction, explanation, framework, or method worth returning to.

### FOLLOW REASON
The next lesson naturally continues the current learning path.

The follow reason must come from course continuity, not from withholding the current answer.

---

## 5. Philosophy 300 is a continuous learning path

The series is not 300 isolated philosophy facts.

Preferred structure:

```
previous lesson establishes a coordinate
→ current lesson solves one question
→ current lesson reaches closure
→ a new question naturally appears
→ next lesson continues the map
```

Example:

001
为什么西方哲学史通常从古希腊开始？

→

002
为什么第一个名字常常是泰勒斯？

→

003
哲学真的从泰勒斯开始就和神话分开了吗？

→

004
什么样的回答才开始算一种“解释”？

→

005
泰勒斯为什么偏偏和“水”联系在一起？

Continuity must not violate lesson closure.

---

## 6. Three-Sentence Retell Test — HARD

Before publication, a zero-baseline reader should be able to answer:

1. 这一讲主要回答了什么？
2. 我今天新知道了什么？
3. 以后我能拿这个知识做什么？

If the third answer is impossible and the lesson offers no other clear save value, revise.

If the first answer differs from the title, revise.

---

## 7. Do not manufacture engagement

Forbidden:
- “99%的人都不知道”
- “原来我们被骗了两千年”
- forced contrarianism
- fake “mind-blowing” framing
- forced method sections
- forced summary tables for saveability
- intentionally withholding the answer to create a cliffhanger
- “下一篇告诉你答案” before current closure

Current lesson must finish its own job.

---

## 8. Length follows value, not quota

There is no hard minimum word count.

Target reading time may guide production, but word count must not create filler.

Hard rule:
> If removing a paragraph does not reduce fact, explanation, distinction, person-understanding, reusable method, or necessary boundary, delete it.

A 1700-character lesson that closes the question is better than a 2400-character lesson with filler.

---

## 9. Lesson Value Gate — REQUIRED INTERNAL CHECK

Before a lesson may reach READY, fill:

```
LESSON:
CENTRAL QUESTION:

KNOW:
读者今天具体知道什么？

CORRECT:
读者修正什么误解？

TOOL:
读者带走什么可复用方法？

PERSON:
如果有人物，本讲最低限度让读者知道他什么？

LIKE REASON:
为什么值得点赞？

SAVE REASON:
为什么值得收藏？

FOLLOW REASON:
为什么会自然想继续下一讲？

NEXT QUESTION:
本讲完整结束后，自然产生的下一问题是什么？

REMOVE TEST:
有哪些段落删掉也不损失信息？
如有 → 删除。
```

If a required field cannot be answered, DRAFT STATUS cannot be READY.

---

## 10. Publication standard

Every lesson must pass all four:

1. **Historical / conceptual accuracy**
2. **Natural Chinese prose**
3. **Central-question closure**
4. **Reader value**

Accuracy alone is not enough.
Readability alone is not enough.
Engagement alone is not enough.

The lesson must do all four.
