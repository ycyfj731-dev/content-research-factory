# Philosophy 300 — Editorial Writing Gate v0.1

Status: HARD GATE

A draft fails if it is accurate but still sounds machine-produced, circular, abstract, or over-explained.

## 1. Directness
- Give the judgment before decorating it.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Avoid “铺垫型” paragraphs that exist only to create atmosphere.
- Do not spend three paragraphs arriving at a point that can be stated in one.

## 2. Progress
Each paragraph must do one identifiable job:
- establish;
- distinguish;
- illustrate;
- qualify;
- connect;
- conclude.

If two consecutive paragraphs do the same job, revise.

## 3. Repetition
Delete:
- semantic restatement;
- emotional restatement;
- “金句版” restatement after the point already landed.

A conclusion may be memorable, but it cannot merely repeat the previous sentence with line breaks.

## 4. Sentence discipline
Prefer:
- one claim;
- one qualification;
- one example

as separate sentences when combining them creates cognitive load.

## 5. Jargon
A beginner should understand the idea before seeing the label.

Bad:
> 泰勒斯在寻找始基。

Better:
> 他在问：这些看起来完全不同的东西，会不会来自同一种更基本的东西？后来的人会用“始基”之类的词讨论这种问题。

## 6. Abstract-language warning
Flag phrases such as:
- 探究方式
- 思想转向
- 解释框架
- 内在秩序
- 根本原则
- 知识谱系
- 认知结构

They are not banned, but they require concrete explanation.

## 7. Rhetorical questions
Use only when the question is the actual philosophical problem.

Do not use questions as empty rhythm devices.

## 8. AI-tic blacklist
Strongly avoid:
- 答案其实没有那么神秘
- 真正重要的是
- 事情开始变得有趣
- 这里有一个关键点
- 让我们先
- 不妨先想一想
- 换句话说 repeated as a crutch
- 这也正是……的地方 repeated as a flourish

## 9. No forced profundity
Do not turn every paragraph into a quotable line.

Most paragraphs should simply explain.

## 10. Final read
Ask:
> If this were printed without the author name, would it read like a person with a clear argument, or like a model performing “good writing”?

If the latter, FAIL.
