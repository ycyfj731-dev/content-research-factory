## Revised canonical hooks

### 007
**阿那克西曼德为什么把万物的开端说成一种没有边界、难以确定的东西？**

Canonical core:
- Later testimony attributes to Anaximander the apeiron as archē.
- The surviving evidence does not let us confidently reconstruct a first-person argument in which he explicitly “rejects Thales’ water.”
- The lesson may explain later ancient reconstructions, but must label them as such.

### 010
**为什么“毕达哥拉斯”和数字后来变得分不开？**

Canonical core:
- Historical Pythagoras is poorly documented.
- Early testimony supports his fame and a distinctive way of life/religious teaching more securely than a fully developed doctrine that “everything is number.”
- Stronger number/cosmos doctrines are associated with later Pythagoreans; the lesson must separate Pythagoras from “Pythagoreanism.”

## Methodological lesson

The course must distinguish three labels in its internal data model:

- **SOURCE CLAIM** — directly or strongly attested.
- **SCHOLARLY RECONSTRUCTION** — reasonable but inferential.
- **COURSE BRIDGE** — our pedagogical question linking one problem to the next.

No COURSE BRIDGE may be written as though it were a philosopher’s explicit historical argument.
