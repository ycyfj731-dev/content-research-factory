# Philosophy 300 — Social Publishing Contract v1.0

Status: **FROZEN**

## Core rule

> **完整正文就是小红书正文；小红书只做分页和视觉编辑，不再额外降密度。**

Philosophy 300 does not maintain a separate simplified Xiaohongshu rewrite by default.

The full approved lesson prose is the social-platform prose unless a later lesson has a specific, evidence-based reason to require adaptation.

## What Xiaohongshu editing may change

- page breaks;
- visual hierarchy;
- typography;
- image/map placement;
- caption placement;
- folio / progress marker such as `002 / 300`;
- cover treatment;
- page-level emphasis;
- source placement where needed for readability.

## What Xiaohongshu editing may not do

- simplify away philosophical terms;
- remove necessary source boundaries;
- shorten the reasoning merely to make the post feel more “social”;
- create a second, lower-density paraphrase of the lesson by default;
- turn complete prose into slogan cards;
- rewrite accurate prose into clickbait;
- delete the lesson’s KNOW / CORRECT / TOOL value;
- replace the approved lesson argument with page-by-page summary language.

## Pagination principle

Each page should perform one **认知动作**.

Typical actions include:
- introduce the person or setting;
- explain a term;
- present a source passage;
- distinguish source from later interpretation;
- correct a misconception;
- answer one part of the central question;
- provide a reusable reading tool;
- close the lesson;
- introduce the next question only after closure.

Pagination exists to improve reading and visual pacing, not to lower intellectual density.

## Relationship to the master lesson

The frozen full lesson remains the single source of truth for:
- wording;
- claims;
- terminology;
- source discipline;
- lesson closure.

The social carousel is a layout of that lesson, not an independent rewrite.

If a future lesson truly requires a shorter platform-specific version, that must be explicitly approved and versioned rather than silently produced.
