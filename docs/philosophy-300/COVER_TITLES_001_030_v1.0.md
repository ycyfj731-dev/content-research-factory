# Philosophy 300 — Cover Titles 001–030 v1.0

Status: **FROZEN**

Purpose: freeze the social-platform cover titles for lectures 001–030.

These titles are downstream of the frozen canonical cores. They may sharpen curiosity and readability, but may not distort the lesson's historical or philosophical core.

## Freeze rules

- Cover title ≠ course title.
- Cover title must be understandable to a zero-baseline reader.
- It may use curiosity, contradiction, or a concrete puzzle.
- It may not invent controversy, exaggerate evidence, or promise something the lesson does not answer.
- Once frozen, later prose/visual work may not silently rewrite these titles.
- Any change requires an explicit version increment.
