## Revised hooks

### 011
**音乐里的整数比例，为什么会让毕达哥拉斯传统把“数”看得如此重要？**

Canonical core:
- Separate historical Pythagoras from later Pythagoreans.
- Use musical ratio evidence cautiously.
- Do not jump from mathematical order to a Platonic “sensible vs intelligible world” distinction.

### 015
**同一条河不断换水，它为什么还是同一条河？——赫拉克利特**

Canonical core:
- Anchor in the best-attested river fragment: the river is “the same” while different waters flow.
- Use this to introduce change-with-order.
- Do not begin by assuming a doctrine of universal flux.

### 017
**巴门尼德为什么把“变化”变成了一个哲学难题？**

Canonical core:
- Introduce the pressure created by thinking and speaking about what-is and what-is-not.
- Do not reduce the poem to “the senses lie.”

### 019
**如果任何东西都不能从“没有”中产生，新的东西从哪里来？**

Canonical core:
- This captures the Eleatic pressure on coming-to-be without assuming that one disputed strict-monist reading is the only reading of Parmenides.
- Opens naturally into pluralist responses.

### 023
**既不让东西凭空产生，又要解释变化，恩培多克勒怎么办？**

Canonical core:
- Four enduring roots plus combination/separation allow change without absolute generation/destruction.
- Mark “response to Parmenides” as a strong historical interpretation, not a verbatim self-description.

### 027
**如果世界由原子和虚空组成，秩序还需要一个“目的”来解释吗？——原子论者**

Canonical core:
- Ancient atomism offers a highly influential non-teleological account of natural order.
- Distinguish Leucippus/Democritus atomism from later mechanistic philosophies.
- Do not infer atheism from non-teleology.

### 028
**为什么到了公元前五世纪，语言、教育和政治越来越重要？——智者登场**

Canonical core:
- Explain the social/political context of professional education, rhetoric and participatory civic life.
- Preserve diversity among sophists.
- Do not narrate a clean replacement of cosmology by “human questions.”

### 030
**为什么柏拉图笔下的苏格拉底总问：“你说的这个词，到底是什么意思？”**

Canonical core:
- Definition-seeking and elenchus are strongly characteristic of Plato’s early dialogues.
- Historical Socrates remains a source problem because Plato, Xenophon and Aristophanes give different portraits.
- The course must distinguish “Plato’s Socrates” from confidently reconstructed biography.

## Cross-cutting rules added by this screen

1. **No textbook slogan as source fact.**
   - “Heraclitus = everything flows”
   - “Parmenides = senses are lies”
   - “Zeno = definitely only defending Parmenides”
   These require source/interpretive qualification.

2. **No retrospective school doctrine assigned to the founder.**
   - Especially Pythagoras / Pythagoreanism.

3. **No clean intellectual rupture unless evidence supports one.**
   - Sophists do not simply replace cosmology with rhetoric.

4. **Historical person != later literary portrait.**
   - Especially Socrates.

5. Preserve internal labels:
   - SOURCE CLAIM
   - SCHOLARLY RECONSTRUCTION
   - COURSE BRIDGE
