# 西方哲学300讲｜Curriculum v0.2

Status: 001–030 CANONICAL CORES FROZEN  
Rule: “300” is a product scale, not a quota. Internal acceptable range remains 280–320, but v0.1 uses 300 slots to test balance.

## Curriculum method

The course uses a **chronological spine + problem-facing front end**.

- Chronological spine: preserve historical dependency, transmission, and the order in which problems change.
- Problem-facing front end: lesson titles are questions, dilemmas, paradoxes, or arguments rather than philosopher biographies.
- Primary-source rule: every lesson gets a source anchor before scripting.
- Canonical-core rule: freeze what the passage is doing before any style rewrite.
- COMPLEX-TO-CLEAR: make the argument intelligible before introducing jargon.
- DISTORTION GATE: no lesson proceeds if source fidelity, attribution, chronology, or explanation consistency fails.
- Style layer comes last.

## Historiographical guardrails

1. **Do not teach a simplistic “myth -> reason” rupture.** Early Greek philosophy develops within, against, and alongside poetic, religious, mathematical, political, and Near Eastern traditions.
2. **“Thales = first philosopher” is a conventional starting point, not an uncontested historical fact.** Much of the picture of the earliest thinkers comes through later sources, especially Aristotle.
3. **Presocratics are fragmentary.** Distinguish direct fragments from testimonia and later reconstruction.
4. **Do not make the history purely Latin-Christian after antiquity.** Medieval philosophy must include Greek/Byzantine, Arabic/Islamic, Jewish, and Latin traditions and their transmissions.
5. **Do not force every period into a march toward modernity.** Each thinker must first be understood in the problems of their own context.
6. **Chronology does not mean isolation.** Insert “problem bridges” showing how a question is inherited, transformed, or rejected.

## First 30 lectures — detailed working map

### 001 — 为什么“西方哲学史”通常从古希腊开始？
**Function:** define the object of the course without pretending Greece emerged in isolation.  
**Core:** “Western philosophy” is a historiographical tradition; Greek thought is a conventional starting point because later Western philosophical traditions repeatedly treated it as an ancestry.  
**Boundary:** do not imply that Greeks invented rational thought from nothing.

### 002 — 为什么泰勒斯被叫作“第一位哲学家”，明明我们几乎没有他的原文？
**Anchor:** Aristotle, *Metaphysics* I.3; later testimonia.  
**Core:** “first philosopher” is a retrospective classification.  
**Boundary:** distinguish Thales from the later Aristotelian vocabulary used to describe him.

### 003 — 哲学真的在某一天取代了神话吗？
**Core:** reject the simplistic mythos-to-logos rupture while explaining the genuine shift toward argument, nature, causes, and public contestability.  
**Boundary:** religion, poetry, and philosophical argument continue to overlap.

### 004 — 什么样的答案，才开始算“解释”？
**Core:** introduce archē, physis, cause, and the move from recounting events to asking what makes them happen.  
**Purpose:** establishes the recurring course question: what counts as a good explanation?

### 005 — 泰勒斯为什么偏偏说“水”？
**Anchor:** Aristotle, *Metaphysics* 983b.  
**Core:** reconstruct cautiously; Aristotle himself speculates about Thales’ reasons.  
**Boundary:** do not pretend we possess Thales’ argument.

### 006 — 如果万物来自一种东西，它变化以后还是同一种东西吗？
**Bridge:** Thales -> Anaximander.  
**Core:** the one-and-many problem emerges.

### 007 — 阿那克西曼德为什么把万物的开端说成一种没有边界、难以确定的东西？
**Anchor:** Anaximander fragment/testimonia.  
**Core:** apeiron as a more abstract originating principle.  
**Boundary:** translations and reconstruction are contested.

### 008 — 世界为什么会自己保持秩序？
**Anchor:** Anaximander’s famous fragment on justice/recompense.  
**Core:** cosmic order without reducing the language of justice to modern physical law.

### 009 — 一口气能解释整个世界吗？——阿那克西美尼
**Core:** air + rarefaction/condensation as a mechanism.  
**Importance:** explanation now includes a process, not merely a basic stuff.

### 010 — 为什么“毕达哥拉斯”和数字后来变得分不开？
**Core:** mathematics, ratio, harmony, way of life.  
**Boundary:** historical Pythagoras and later Pythagorean doctrine are difficult to separate.

### 011 — 音乐里的整数比例，为什么会让毕达哥拉斯传统把“数”看得如此重要？
**Bridge:** Pythagorean mathematics -> later rationalist tendencies.  
**Boundary:** do not retroject Plato wholesale into early Pythagoreanism.

### 012 — 如果牛会画画，它们会把神画成牛吗？——色诺芬尼
**Anchor:** Xenophanes fragments on anthropomorphic gods.  
**Core:** criticism of projection in religion.

### 013 — 即使真相存在，人能确定自己知道吗？——色诺芬尼
**Core:** epistemic limits and the difference between truth and justified certainty.  
**Boundary:** do not simply label Xenophanes a modern skeptic.

### 014 — “人不能两次踏进同一条河”真是赫拉克利特说的吗？
**Core:** use the famous river tradition to teach source discipline.  
**Purpose:** first explicit lesson on quotation vs later paraphrase.

### 015 — 同一条河不断换水，它为什么还是同一条河？——赫拉克利特
**Core:** change, order, logos.

### 016 — 两个相反的东西怎么可能属于同一个世界？
**Anchor:** Heraclitean unity/tension of opposites.  
**Core:** opposition can constitute order rather than merely destroy it.

### 017 — 巴门尼德为什么把“变化”变成了一个哲学难题？
**Core:** transition from Heraclitus-style change problem to Eleatic being.

### 018 — “不存在的东西”你能想吗？——巴门尼德最危险的一步
**Anchor:** *On Nature* fragments.  
**Core:** thinking/speaking of what-is and what-is-not.

### 019 — 如果任何东西都不能从“没有”中产生，新的东西从哪里来？
**Core:** consequences of Parmenidean being.  
**Boundary:** distinguish poem’s argument from later textbook slogans.

### 020 — 理性和眼睛冲突时，该信谁？
**Core:** reason/sense conflict sharpened by Eleatic argument.

### 021 — 阿喀琉斯为什么永远追不上乌龟？——芝诺
**Core:** paradox as argument, not puzzle entertainment.

### 022 — 芝诺真想证明“我们根本不能走路”吗？
**Core:** what the paradoxes were meant to defend and what later mathematics changes.  
**Boundary:** do not use calculus as if it simply makes the philosophical issue disappear.

### 023 — 既不让东西凭空产生，又要解释变化，恩培多克勒怎么办？
**Core:** no absolute coming-to-be; rearrangement of enduring roots.

### 024 — 爱与争斗怎么成了宇宙的力量？——恩培多克勒
**Core:** four roots + Love/Strife.  
**Boundary:** preserve the overlap of cosmology, religion, and poetry rather than modernizing the terms.

### 025 — 每样东西里都有一点所有东西？——阿那克萨戈拉
**Core:** mixture and “everything in everything.”

### 026 — 如果万物混在一起，谁让世界开始转动？——Nous
**Core:** Anaxagorean Mind as initiating/order-producing principle.  
**Boundary:** do not equate nous straightforwardly with a creator God.

### 027 — 如果世界由原子和虚空组成，秩序还需要一个“目的”来解释吗？——原子论者
**Core:** atomism and void; mechanistic explanation.

### 028 — 为什么到了公元前五世纪，语言、教育和政治越来越重要？——智者登场
**Core:** Athens, rhetoric, civic life, education; shift of emphasis rather than clean replacement of cosmology.

### 029 — “人是万物的尺度”到底是什么意思？——普罗泰戈拉
**Core:** perception, relativism, interpretation debates.  
**Boundary:** do not reduce it to “everyone has their own truth.”

### 030 — 为什么柏拉图笔下的苏格拉底总问：“你说的这个词，到底是什么意思？”
**Core:** entry into Socratic ethical inquiry and definition-seeking.  
**Source problem:** Plato, Xenophon, Aristophanes are different witnesses; “the historical Socrates” cannot simply be read off one dialogue.  
**Next:** opens the Socrates/Plato block.

## Source framework for building the full map

Use as structural references, not as authorities to copy mechanically:

- Anthony Kenny, *A New History of Western Philosophy* (Oxford): chronological narrative + thematic analysis.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: philosopher/problem articles and bibliographies.
- Cambridge histories/companions: period-specific historiography and source problems.
- Primary texts whenever extant.
- For Presocratics: distinguish fragments from testimonia; use modern collections such as Laks–Most where possible.
