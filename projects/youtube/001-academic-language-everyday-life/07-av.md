## Visual strategy

Primary visual families:
1. Real documents / article pages / source excerpts
2. Search interfaces / query-path recreations
3. Original editorial diagrams
4. Editorial typography
5. Licensed or self-shot everyday-life B-roll
6. Limited abstract AI motion for transitions only

House rules:
- No generic sad-person-on-phone filler.
- No fake social posts, fake clinical screenshots, or fake historical documents.
- No AI-generated “ADHD person,” “narcissist,” therapist, feminist activist, abuse survivor, or historical figure presented as evidence.
- Hypothetical scenes must be visibly presented as illustration, not documentation.
- Statistics only appear as sourced graphics with sample/context labels.
- Do not change image every sentence. Hold a visual idea long enough for the viewer to understand it.
