## Asset policy

Priority order:
1. official / primary-source documents and pages
2. original diagrams, typography and recreated charts from verified data
3. public-domain or clearly licensed archival material
4. limited editorial screenshots where the source itself is being discussed
5. licensed/self-shot everyday B-roll
6. generated visuals only for hypothetical or abstract transitions where no evidence claim is implied

Never use:
- downloaded TikTok/YouTube/influencer clips merely as decoration;
- AI-generated historical footage;
- fake posts or fake clinical interfaces;
- generated “ADHD person,” “narcissist,” therapist, abuse survivor or feminist activist as evidence;
- unlabeled recreated interfaces that could be mistaken for real screenshots.

Rights status vocabulary:
- **GREEN** — original, public-domain, or clearly licensed for intended use
- **YELLOW** — editorial/document screenshot or archive item requiring item-level review
- **RED** — do not use without explicit license/permission
- **TBD** — source not yet selected/acquired

## Rights / evidence notes by source family

### Official and academic webpages
Page screenshots are **YELLOW**, not automatically “free to use.” Keep crops brief, source-identifying and tied directly to commentary. Where the visual value is low, prefer re-typeset short factual wording with a visible citation.

### Charts
Do not copy publisher figures when the underlying values can be responsibly recreated. A13 should be an original chart with the study title/source in the lower-third/source line.

### Search interfaces
A09/A17/A28 are original editorial reconstructions. Never place fake user names, fake engagement numbers, fake search rankings or fabricated quotations into them.

### Archives
A30/A31/A38 require item-level review. “Old” does not equal public domain. Record creator, year, archive URL, rights statement and required attribution for the exact item used.

### B-roll
No commercial stock enters the final edit until its actual license and intended YouTube/commercial-use terms are recorded. Watermarked preview footage is planning-only.
