## Frozen explanation core

### Supported
1. The public circulation of therapeutic/expert concepts is real and predates TikTok.
2. ADHD, narcissist, patriarchy and PUA have materially different origin paths.
3. Popularization can produce self-recognition, lay appropriation and semantic expansion.
4. Meaning change during circulation is documented in multiple cases.
5. The internet is a circulation environment, not a sufficient single causal explanation.

### Strength
- **Strong:** origin histories, clinical boundaries, selected ADHD findings.
- **Moderate:** semantic migration and public-language interpretation.
- **Limited:** any general claim about algorithms, prevalence across populations, or synchronized timing across terms.

### Scope
This is a cultural essay built from selected cases. It does not estimate how all people speak, diagnose a generation, or measure a universal platform effect.

### Boundaries
- Do not diagnose viewers or third parties.
- Do not imply ADHD is merely an identity or trend.
- Do not treat “narcissist” and NPD as interchangeable.
- Do not treat patriarchy's digital resurgence as proof of a single political cause.
- Do not equate PUA's Chinese semantic expansion with one universally fixed definition.
- Do not say “the algorithm” caused the phenomenon.
