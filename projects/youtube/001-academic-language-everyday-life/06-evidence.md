## Frozen explanation core

### Supported
- The public circulation of expert/therapeutic concepts is real and not historically unprecedented.
- The four selected cases have different origins and therefore disprove a simple “academia → TikTok” pipeline.
- Meaning change during popularization is documented in several cases.
- Semantic expansion is an established research topic for harm/mental-health concepts.
- Lay appropriation of social-scientific concepts is a recognized sociological process.

### Strength
Moderate-to-strong for the existence of concept migration and semantic broadening.  
Weak-to-moderate for any single universal causal mechanism explaining all cases.

### Scope
The episode is about **selected cultural cases**, not a population-wide estimate of how “people today” speak.

### Boundaries
- Do not diagnose viewers.
- Do not imply ADHD is merely a social-media identity.
- Do not treat broadening of “patriarchy” or “PUA” as equivalent to clinical concept creep.
- Do not say algorithms caused the trend without direct evidence.
- Do not say all semantic change is dilution or misuse.

## Rejected claims

| Rejected wording | Reason |
|---|---|
| “These words all came from academia.” | Factually false: gaslighting and PUA do not fit |
| “TikTok made these words popular.” | Overbroad causal claim |
| “Algorithms reward self-diagnosis.” | Current evidence is correlational; algorithm mechanism not directly observed |
| “Everyone is diagnosing themselves now.” | Unsupported universalization |
| “Patriarchy became popular because of Taylor Swift/Barbie.” | These may be cultural markers, not demonstrated causes |
| “PUA simply means gaslighting in China.” | Too absolute; usage is broader/variable |

## Open evidence gaps

1. Comparable historical time-series data across ADHD, gaslighting, patriarchy and PUA.
2. Direct platform-mechanism evidence showing algorithmic amplification of named concepts.
3. Full-text access to Hill & Allen for any detail beyond the abstract.
4. Publisher-verified full text for the Douban/PUA semantic-shift study before direct quotation.
5. Broader competitor sample from established YouTube essay channels.
