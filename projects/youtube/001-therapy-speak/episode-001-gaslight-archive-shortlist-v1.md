**Film identification:** BFI confirms George Cukor's 1944 *Gaslight* featuring Ingrid Bergman (https://www.bfi.org.uk/film/7b140561-04e4-597a-adc0-13f8a539287e/gaslight).

**Shot feasibility:** Two authentic 1944 publicity candidates exist, but no proven 1938 premiere image has been approved. A transition may place a self-typeset *1938 Gas Light — Patrick Hamilton* title beside a genuine 1944 still, with no claim that the 1938 typography is a period artifact. Documentary quality depends on better archival evidence and material-driven framing rather than old-paper effects.

**Rights gate:** Public-domain claims on Wikimedia Commons are not proof of world-wide clearance; verify intended distribution jurisdictions and source authenticity. Film frames/posters may have different rights from separate publicity photographs. The Commons retouching CC0 declaration applies to restoration only, not automatically to the underlying photograph.

**Next production step:** Retrieve one of the 1944 candidates at original resolution; inspect image sharpness and framing; approve it only after rights review. Seek a genuine 1938 print programme/library collection. Do not reuse 1951 or 1960s visual as a 1938 original. Then prepare two high-fidelity style frames, not a slide deck.
