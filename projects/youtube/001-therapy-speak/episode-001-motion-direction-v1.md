## Referenced design method

The Prompt Motion example [Shape morphing through UI states](https://prompt-motion.com/twoclipping-5cba86) explicitly advocates: one persisting element rather than cut-to-cut replacements, absolute-time `seek(t)`, independent text entrance/exit, restrained springs and no gratuitous glows/particles, reviewing key-state frames before rendering. **Adaptation, not copying:** its BPM/music-led loops are NOT suitable for this narrated essay; all visual revelations follow spoken phrases.

**Mandatory distinction:** seconds below delimit words spoken within the coverage unit; they are **not** independently verified animation events. Animation triggers must use the matching sentence or word start from the verified alignment JSON; if an exact word time is missing, use the verified sentence start and do not claim word-level precision.

## Reusable engineering primitives

1. `PersistentToken`: world-space text identity reparented between neutral frames; never fabricate underlying dialogue. Do not use this for terms that differ semantically.
2. `EvidenceCard`: author/year/scope/source qualifier with independent reveal and safe hold; can stay static when VO is information-dense.
3. `ComparisonRail`: matched text baselines for same-example contrasts; do not animate one comparison as an empirical result.
4. `ContextField`: blank unknown evidence space and neutral audience geometry, always illustrative, no realistic social screenshots.
5. `WorkweekCalendar`: script-locked hours/weekend/access depiction with no fictional employer actions.
6. `CaptionSafeZone`: lower 14% of frame reserved from main motion typography; subtitles generated separately from SRT.

### Deterministic motion spec

- Render at 1920×1080, 30 fps initially; every visual parameter defined by absolute time `stateAt(t)`; no accumulating per-frame state.
- Use restrained cubic/bezier eases for informational transitions, optional spring settling for one object with low overshoot. No blanket transition preset per slide.
- Avoid line-by-line captions in the main animation; research cards must remain readable long enough and not conflict with lower subtitle area.
- Generate and inspect initial/semantic-reveal/exit golden frames for each unit, including all 34 boundaries, before a full MP4.
- **No score for quality from 'render successful'.** A rendered but visually underdesigned slide montage is a rejected deliverable.

## Production acceptance gates

- Script → screen fidelity: all on-screen claims tied to exact spoken passage; no added story.
- Semantic triggers: every newly meaningful element appears at or after the corresponding audio phrase.
- Art direction: real layered assets, visual hierarchy, informative movement; not just fading outlined rectangles.
- Output: 34 motion clips or 1 contiguous time-driven canvas, 14:20 narration, exact SRT. **Not yet rendered.**
