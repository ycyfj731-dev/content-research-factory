## Slide composition constraints

- Build static 16:9 **editable** key frames for each content state. Use distinct slide numbers per key state but retain VC ID. A VC may occupy multiple states; it is not an arbitrary single PowerPoint slide.
- Every visible phrase, numerical figure and documentary signal needs a supporting spoken phrase or independently verified source **specifically named in that passage**. Never pre-display a conclusion scheduled for later sentences.
- For visual representation of abstract/conditional claims, keep imagery non-documentary and free from invented factual specificity. No fake screenshots and no pseudo-documentary camera framing.
- References (APA, Merriam-Webster, WHO and named academic studies) may use original source **identifiers**; screenshot, quoted article text, logos or film material remain rights-pending.
- Neutral shapes, typography, alignment, masks, geometrical movement and recurring framing can achieve production quality without inserting extra factual claims. Character illustrations (including border collie) must not enact independent events beyond what VO describes.
- For each slide record: VC, paragraph, trigger phrase, source/asset ID, layout state, motion hand-off, lower-caption-safe region, factual provenance, and ‘no extra claims’ check.
